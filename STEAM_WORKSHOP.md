# Steam Workshop Page

## Title

Craftable Ancient Pseudoscience Station

## Description

Adds craftable Ancient Pseudoscience Station recipes and configurable Thulecite Fragment crafting.

Features:
- Craft the Ancient Pseudoscience Station / Small Station variants.
- Configure station crafting difficulty: Disabled, Easy, Normal, or Hard.
- Configure Thulecite Fragment crafting difficulty: Disabled, Easy, Normal, or Hard.
- Supports the modern Don't Starve Together crafting menu.
